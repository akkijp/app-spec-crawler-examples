About this screen

Purpose

Where comments left on posts are approved, rejected, answered, edited or removed before and after they appear on the public site.

Shown when

Shown when "Comments" is chosen in the side menu or the comment icon in the admin toolbar (/wp-admin/edit-comments.php). The menu badge shows "1" while one comment awaits moderation.

Who uses it and how

Editors and administrators review new comments, approve or reject the pending ones, reply from the admin area, and clean up spam.

Main features

  • Status views with counts (All, Mine, Pending, Approved, Spam, Trash)
  • Keyword search with "Search Comments"
  • Filtering by comment type
  • Sorting by author, post and submission date
  • Per-comment Approve/Unapprove, Reply, Quick Edit, Edit, Spam, Trash
  • Bulk approve, unapprove, mark as spam and move to trash

Usage flow

  1. Open "Comments" (the badge shows how many are pending).
  2. Read the highlighted pending comment.
  3. Hover the row and choose "Approve", "Reply", "Spam" or "Trash".
  4. For many comments, tick their checkboxes, choose a bulk action and press "Apply".
  5. Use the status views to review "Pending", "Spam" or "Trash".

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured in the "All" view with one pending and two approved comments. The inline reply and "Quick Edit" forms, the empty state and the Spam/Trash views (which probably offer restore and permanent delete) were not captured.

Business rules and constraints

A comment is Pending, Approved, Spam or Trash. Only approved comments are counted in the grey bubble; pending ones are shown in the red badge. The side-menu and toolbar badges count pending comments.

Open questions

  • Under which conditions a new comment is held for moderation rather than approved automatically (probably set in Discussion Settings).
  • Whether "Spam" and "Trash" act immediately or ask for confirmation.
  • Whether replying to a pending comment also approves it.
