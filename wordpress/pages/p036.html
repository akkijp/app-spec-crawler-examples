About this screen

Purpose

Lets a category be renamed, re-slugged, moved under another category, described, or deleted; reached from the Categories list.

Shown when

Shown when a category name or its "Edit" action is chosen on the Categories screen (/wp-admin/term.php?taxonomy=category&tag_ID=2). Probably limited to users who may manage categories.

Who uses it and how

Editors and administrators correct a category's name or URL slug, reorganise the hierarchy, or remove a category that is no longer needed.

Main features

  • Editing a category's name, slug, parent and description
  • Deleting the category
  • "View Category" link in the admin toolbar

Usage flow

  1. On the Categories screen, click a category name.
  2. Change the name, slug, parent or description.
  3. Press "Update" to save, or "Delete" to remove the category.
  4. Return to the Categories list through the side menu.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Only the initial form for "Announcements" was captured. The success notice after "Update", validation errors and the form for the default category "Uncategorized" (which probably has no "Delete" link) were not captured. There are no "Screen Options" or "Help" tabs on this screen.

Business rules and constraints

A category cannot be its own parent (the edited category is missing from the parent options). Per the note on the Categories screen, deleting a category moves posts that were only in it to "Uncategorized".

Open questions

  • Whether "Delete" shows a confirmation dialog.
  • What message and navigation follow a successful "Update".
  • Whether changing the slug redirects the old category URL.
