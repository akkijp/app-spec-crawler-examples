About this screen

Purpose

Discloses to site owners what the software reports to WordPress.org. It is separate from the site's own privacy policy, which is handled on p032 Privacy Settings.

Shown when

Shown to any logged-in user who opens it from the WordPress logo menu in the toolbar ("About WordPress") or from another About tab; the side menu has no item for it, so no menu entry is highlighted.

Who uses it and how

Any administrator or editor of the site who wants background on the WordPress software itself; it is read once in a while, not used in daily work. It is also useful to whoever writes the site's privacy policy or answers a security questionnaire.

Main features

  • Disclosure of data sent to WordPress.org
  • Links to the statistics page and the WordPress.org privacy policy

Usage flow

  1. Open "About WordPress" and choose the "Privacy" tab.
  2. Read what is sent and follow the link to the WordPress.org privacy policy for details.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

A static page with no variants.

Business rules and constraints

The page has the same title, "Privacy", as the settings screen p032 but is unrelated to it: this one concerns the software's communication with WordPress.org, while p032 concerns the site's own policy page.
