About this screen

Purpose

The working list for managing the site's fixed pages (About, Contact, Pricing and so on); the entry point to editing a page or creating a new one.

Shown when

Shown when "Pages" > "All Pages" is chosen in the side menu (/wp-admin/edit.php?post_type=page). Probably limited to users who may edit pages.

Who uses it and how

Editors and administrators find a page by title or status, check who wrote it and when it was published, then open it for editing or act on several pages at once.

Main features

  • Status views with counts (All, Mine, Published, Draft)
  • Keyword search with "Search Pages"
  • Filtering by month
  • Sorting by title, comment count and date
  • Bulk edit and bulk move to trash
  • Row actions: Edit, Quick Edit, Trash, View/Preview
  • "Add Page" button

Usage flow

  1. Open "Pages" > "All Pages".
  2. Narrow the list with a status view, the search box or the date filter.
  3. Click a page title to open it in the editor.
  4. For several pages, tick their checkboxes, choose a bulk action and press "Apply".
  5. Press "Add Page" to create a new page.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured in the default "All" view with 6 pages on a single page of results. Filtered views, the empty state, child pages (which are probably shown indented under their parent), "Quick Edit" and the trash view were not captured.

Business rules and constraints

Pages have no categories or tags. One page can be designated as the site's privacy policy page and is labelled "Privacy Policy Page" in the list. "Mine (2)" matches the two pages whose author is the logged-in user.

Open questions

  • Why four published pages have no author (possibly created by a script or a since-deleted user).
  • Whether "Privacy Policy Draft", which is published, is intentionally separate from the draft "Privacy Policy" page.
  • How many rows are shown per page before pagination appears.
