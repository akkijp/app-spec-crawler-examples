About this screen

Purpose

Gives a logged-in user a one-screen overview of content volume, recent publishing and comment activity, and shortcuts into the areas that need attention (the pending comment, drafts, the site editor).

Shown when

Shown at /wp-admin/ after login and when "Dashboard" > "Home" is chosen in the side menu. It is probably not shown to visitors who are not logged in (the login screen was not captured).

Who uses it and how

Site administrators and editors open it first to see what happened recently, moderate the newest comments in place, jot down a draft, and jump to posts, pages or comments through the count links.

Main features

  • Welcome panel with links to add a page, open the site editor and edit styles
  • Content counts for posts, pages and comments in "At a Glance"
  • Recently published posts and recent comments with inline moderation actions
  • "Quick Draft" form and "Your Recent Drafts" list
  • Site health summary and WordPress events/news feed
  • Widget arrangement controls ("Move up", "Move down", "Show or hide panel") and "Screen Options"

Usage flow

  1. Open the admin area; the Dashboard is shown.
  2. Read "At a Glance" and "Activity" to see content counts and what was published or commented most recently.
  3. If a comment is marked "[Pending]", hover it and approve, reply, mark as spam or trash it, or open the Comments screen through "1 Comment in moderation".
  4. Optionally type a title and content in "Quick Draft" and press "Save Draft".
  5. Follow a count link or the side menu to the list screen that needs work.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured with the welcome panel still shown, site health reporting "Should be improved" with 7 items, one pending comment and one recent draft. With no pending comments the highlighted row and the "1" badges in the side menu and toolbar would presumably disappear. Widgets can probably be collapsed, reordered or hidden per user.

Business rules and constraints

The comment count in "At a Glance" (2) appears to count approved comments only, with the pending one reported separately (inferred from the totals 3 = 2 approved + 1 pending). "Recently Published" appears to list the five newest published posts only.

Open questions

  • Whether dismissing the welcome panel is remembered per user or for the whole site.
  • Which widgets are shown to roles other than the logged-in administrator.
  • What "Site Health Status" shows before the checks have run or when no issues are found.
