About this screen

Purpose

The working list for finding, reviewing and managing posts; the entry point to editing a post or creating a new one.

Shown when

Shown when "Posts" > "All Posts" is chosen in the side menu (/wp-admin/edit.php). Users who cannot edit posts probably cannot open it.

Who uses it and how

Authors and editors use it to find a post by title, status, month or category, check who wrote it and how many comments it has, then open it for editing or act on several posts at once.

Main features

  • Status views with counts (All, Mine, Published, Draft, Pending)
  • Keyword search with "Search Posts"
  • Filtering by month and by category
  • Sorting by title, comment count and date
  • Bulk edit and bulk move to trash
  • Row actions: Edit, Quick Edit, Trash, View/Preview
  • "Add Post" button

Usage flow

  1. Open "Posts" > "All Posts".
  2. Narrow the list with a status view, the search box or the date and category filters.
  3. Click a post title (or "Edit" in the row actions) to open it in the editor.
  4. For several posts, tick their checkboxes, choose a bulk action and press "Apply".
  5. Press "Add Post" to write a new post.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured in the default "All" view with 9 posts on a single page. Filtered views, search results, the empty state, the "Quick Edit" inline form and the trash view were not captured. A "Trash" status view probably appears once a post has been trashed.

Business rules and constraints

Posts carry one of at least three statuses here: Published (7), Draft (1) and Pending (1). "Mine (3)" matches the three posts whose author is the logged-in user. Eight posts are tagged "howto, release"; only "Hello world!" has no tags and is in "Uncategorized".

Open questions

  • How many rows are shown per page before pagination appears.
  • Whether "Trash" in the row actions asks for confirmation.
  • Which fields "Quick Edit" and "Bulk edit" expose.
