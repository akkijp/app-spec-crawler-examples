About this screen

Purpose

How an administrator gives a new person access to the site with the right role. It is "Users" > "Add User" in the side menu.

Shown when

Opened from "Users" > "Add User", the "Add User" button on p015 Users, or "New" > "User" in the toolbar. Shown to the logged-in user "demo" (an Administrator); whether lower roles can open it was not captured.

Who uses it and how

A site administrator onboarding a new writer, editor or subscriber; they fill in username and email, pick a role and submit.

Main features

  • Create a user account
  • Generate or type a password with a strength indicator
  • Send the new user a notification email
  • Assign a role at creation

Usage flow

  1. Open "Users" > "Add User".
  2. Enter the username and email, and optionally name and website.
  3. Set the password (the password row was hidden in this capture).
  4. Decide whether to send the notification email and choose the role.
  5. Press "Add User".

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Only the empty initial form was captured, with the password row hidden. Validation errors (missing or duplicate username/email), the weak-password confirmation and the success message were not captured.

Business rules and constraints

New accounts default to the least-privileged role "Subscriber". Unlike the role dropdowns on p015 and p045, there is no "— No role for this site —" option here.

Open questions

  • The password field was hidden in this capture, so its initial content and the look of the strength indicator were not confirmed.
  • Does the notification email contain the password or a link to set one?
  • Which characters are allowed in a username?
  • Is the default role configurable (General Settings)?
