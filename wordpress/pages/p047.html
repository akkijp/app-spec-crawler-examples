About this screen

Purpose

Helps the site owner write the privacy policy by supplying a section-by-section template and suggested wording, which they copy into the page designated on p032 Privacy Settings.

Shown when

Shown when the "Policy Guide" tab of "Settings" → "Privacy" is chosen (the address carries tab=policyguide). Both panels start collapsed. Probably limited to administrators.

Who uses it and how

The site owner or whoever drafts the legal pages reads the template while editing the privacy policy page, and comes back after adding themes or plugins to look for new suggested text.

Main features

  • Introduction to writing a privacy policy
  • Collapsible privacy policy template
  • Collapsible policy text suggested by WordPress
  • Tab switch between "Settings" and "Policy Guide"

Usage flow

  1. Open "Settings" → "Privacy" and choose the "Policy Guide" tab.
  2. Expand "Privacy Policy Guide" and read the section summaries.
  3. Expand "WordPress" under "Policies" and copy the suggested text that applies.
  4. Return to "Settings", open the policy page with "Edit" and paste and adapt the text.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Only the all-collapsed state was captured. The expanded panels and any copy control inside them were not captured. The number of panels under "Policies" probably grows with installed themes and plugins.

Business rules and constraints

According to the introduction, writing a comprehensive policy that meets national and international requirements remains the site owner's responsibility; the guide only suggests content. Once published, the policy page should be added to the navigation menu.
