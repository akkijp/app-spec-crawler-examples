About this screen

Purpose

Defines how the site names itself and how dates and times are shown everywhere else. It is the first item of the "Settings" menu and is normally set once when the site is created.

Shown when

Shown to a logged-in user who can manage site options (the capture was taken as the user "demo"); other roles were not captured, so they probably do not see the Settings menu.

Who uses it and how

The site administrator changes these values at setup time or when the site is renamed, moved to another address, or handed to a different contact person. Reached from the "Settings" submenu in the side menu; the sibling settings screens are p025 General Settings, p026 Connectors, p027 Writing Settings, p028 Reading Settings, p029 Discussion Settings, p030 Media Settings, p031 Permalink Settings and p032 Privacy Settings.

Main features

  • Site title and tagline
  • Site icon selection
  • WordPress address and site address
  • Administration email address with confirmation
  • Open registration switch and default role for new users
  • Site language, timezone, date format, time format, first day of the week

Usage flow

  1. Open "Settings" → "General".
  2. Edit the title, tagline or other fields.
  3. Check the "Preview:" lines if the date or time format was changed.
  4. Press "Save Changes".
  5. If the administration email was changed, confirm it from the email sent to the new address.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Only the saved, untouched state was captured. The success notice after saving, validation errors, the pending-confirmation notice for a changed email address and the state with a site icon set were not captured.

Business rules and constraints

New accounts created through self-registration would get the role chosen in "New User Default Role", which only matters when "Anyone can register" is ticked. The date and time formats chosen here are probably used across the admin and the public site.

Open questions

  • Why does "New User Default Role" list only three roles in the crawl record, and are more roles offered in practice?
  • What is shown after saving, and which fields are validated on the server?
  • Is the page reloaded in the new language right after "Site Language" is changed?
