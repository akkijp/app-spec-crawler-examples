About this screen

Purpose

Lets a tag be renamed, re-slugged, described, or deleted; reached from the Tags list.

Shown when

Shown when a tag name or its "Edit" action is chosen on the Tags screen (/wp-admin/term.php?taxonomy=post_tag&tag_ID=8). Probably limited to users who may manage tags.

Who uses it and how

Editors and administrators fix a tag's spelling or URL slug, add a description, or remove a tag.

Main features

  • Editing a tag's name, slug and description
  • Deleting the tag
  • "View Tag" link in the admin toolbar

Usage flow

  1. On the Tags screen, click a tag name.
  2. Change the name, slug or description.
  3. Press "Update" to save, or "Delete" to remove the tag.
  4. Return to the Tags list through the side menu.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Only the initial form for "howto" was captured. The success notice after "Update" and validation errors were not captured. There are no "Screen Options" or "Help" tabs on this screen.

Business rules and constraints

Tags are flat: they have no parent. The tag shown is attached to 6 counted posts according to the Tags list, so deleting it would probably remove it from those posts.

Open questions

  • Whether "Delete" shows a confirmation dialog.
  • What message and navigation follow a successful "Update".
