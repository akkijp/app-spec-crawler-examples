About this screen

Purpose

Maintains the hierarchical classification used to organise posts; categories created here appear in the post editor and in the category filter of the posts list.

Shown when

Shown when "Posts" > "Categories" is chosen in the side menu (/wp-admin/edit-tags.php?taxonomy=category). Probably limited to users who may manage categories.

Who uses it and how

Editors and administrators add a category, check how many posts each one holds, and open a category to rename or delete it.

Main features

  • Adding a category with optional slug, parent and description
  • Listing categories with slug and post count
  • Keyword search with "Search Categories"
  • Bulk delete and per-row Edit / Quick Edit / Delete / View
  • Link to the "category to tag converter"

Usage flow

  1. Open "Posts" > "Categories".
  2. To add one, fill in "Name" (and optionally "Slug", "Parent Category", "Description") and press "Add Category".
  3. To change one, click its name in the table to open the edit screen.
  4. To remove several, tick their checkboxes, choose "Delete" in "Bulk actions" and press "Apply".

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured with five flat (non-nested) categories and an empty add form. Child categories, validation errors on the add form, search results and the "Quick Edit" inline form were not captured.

Business rules and constraints

"Uncategorized" is the default category and cannot be deleted (stated on screen). The counts sum to 7, which matches the number of published posts, so "Count" appears to count published posts only (inferred).

Open questions

  • Whether "Delete" in the row actions asks for confirmation.
  • What error is shown when a duplicate name or slug is entered.
  • Whether the default category can be changed elsewhere (probably in Writing Settings).
