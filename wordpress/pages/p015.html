About this screen

Purpose

The place where an administrator sees who has access to the site and with which role, and from which accounts are edited, deleted or given a new role. It is "Users" > "All Users" in the side menu.

Shown when

Opened from "Users" > "All Users". Shown to the logged-in user "demo" (an Administrator); whether lower roles can open it was not captured.

Who uses it and how

A site administrator reviewing accounts, changing roles when responsibilities change, or sending a password reset to someone who is locked out.

Main features

  • List users with role and post count
  • Filter by role
  • Search users
  • Sort by username or email
  • Bulk delete or send password resets
  • Bulk change role
  • Open a user for editing

Usage flow

  1. Open "Users" from the side menu.
  2. Filter by role or search to find the account.
  3. Click the username to edit the user, or use a row action.
  4. For several users, tick the checkboxes and use "Bulk actions" or "Change role to…".
  5. Press "Add User" to create a new account.

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured with 4 users on one page. Search results, a filtered view, an empty result and pagination (more users than one page) were not captured. Row actions are hidden until hover.

Business rules and constraints

A user cannot delete their own account or send themselves a reset from this list, judging by the shorter row actions for "demo". Deleting a user who has posts probably asks what to do with those posts.

Open questions

  • What happens to the 3 posts of author1 if that user is deleted?
  • How many users are shown per page?
  • Can roles other than Administrator see this list?
