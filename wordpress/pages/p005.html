About this screen

Purpose

Maintains the flat keyword labels attached to posts; the counterpart of Categories without a hierarchy.

Shown when

Shown when "Posts" > "Tags" is chosen in the side menu (/wp-admin/edit-tags.php?taxonomy=post_tag). Probably limited to users who may manage tags.

Who uses it and how

Editors and administrators add tags ahead of use, see which tags are unused, and open a tag to rename or delete it.

Main features

  • Adding a tag with optional slug and description
  • Listing tags with slug and post count
  • Keyword search with "Search Tags"
  • Bulk delete and per-row Edit / Quick Edit / Delete / View
  • Link to the "tag to category converter"

Usage flow

  1. Open "Posts" > "Tags".
  2. To add one, fill in "Name" (and optionally "Slug", "Description") and press "Add Tag".
  3. To change one, click its name in the table to open the edit screen.
  4. To remove several, tick their checkboxes, choose "Delete" in "Bulk actions" and press "Apply".

Variants and business rules

States and variants

Captured with four tags, two of them unused (count 0), and an empty add form. Validation errors, search results and the "Quick Edit" inline form were not captured.

Business rules and constraints

Tags have no hierarchy (no parent field). "howto" and "release" each show 6 although 8 posts in the posts list carry them, so "Count" appears to count published posts only, excluding the draft and the pending post (inferred).

Open questions

  • Whether "Delete" in the row actions asks for confirmation.
  • What happens to posts when a tag in use is deleted (probably the tag is simply removed from them).
